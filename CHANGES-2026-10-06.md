# 2026-10-06 — ETF trend redesign

## Why

The 2026-10-05 review found the stock strategy (SMA 20/50 on 32 hand-picked mega-caps) scored a Sharpe of 0.29 against 1.08 for buy-and-hold in its own learning run. Four further problems:

- **Hindsight universe:** the stocks were picked knowing they became winners.
- **Backtest/live mismatch:** live trading used intraday partial bars and entered up to 3 days late.
- **Jev added noise:** the same TXN setup scored confidence 0.12 → 0.67 in 20 minutes.
- **Unreachable readiness gate:** it effectively needed about 2.7 years of data.

The redesign moves to a strategy with decades of independent evidence (Faber 2007; Hurst, Ooi & Pedersen 2017; Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen 2012). It was tested in `scripts/etf_trend_backtest.py` with:

- next-day fills, costs and T-bill interest on cash
- 1997–2026 data (index-fund proxies before each ETF launched) and 2007–2026 (real ETFs)
- sensitivity grids, rebalance-day timing luck, walk-forward and a block bootstrap

## Before → after

| | Before (stock_signals) | After (etf_trend) |
|---|---|---|
| Universe | 32 mega-cap stocks | 9 asset-class ETFs + BIL for cash |
| Signal | SMA 20/50 cross on daily bars, checked every 5 min on the unfinished bar | Price vs 10-month average, ±1% buffer, completed closes only |
| Trading | Any 5-min cycle; entries up to 3 days after a signal | Tranche days 1, 6, 11, 16 → filled after the next open |
| Sizing | 5% per stock, 6 positions, 30% max invested | Inverse-volatility weights, ≤ 35% per ETF, up to 100% invested, no leverage |
| Stops | 3×ATR broker stops | None (the monthly rule exits; tested stops all made results worse) |
| Jev (LLM) | Confidence gate could veto entries | Shadow only: reviews entries after they are placed, logged and scored, never blocks |
| Advisor (Kimi) | Called on signals | Not called in this mode |
| Learning | 3 random mutations a day, judged against the current strategy | Monitor: paper vs model tracking, vs SPY and 60/40; fixed parameters (walk-forward re-tuning did worse) |
| Readiness | 126 days, 40 trades, Sharpe ≥ 1, PSR ≥ 0.95 | 252 days, tracking error ≤ 3%, drawdown ≤ 20% and no deeper than 60/40 |
| Data | Alpaca IEX bars (2–3% of volume) | yfinance adjusted consolidated closes (Alpaca fallback), cached once a day |
| Kill switch | 15% drawdown, daily loss 3% | 20% below peak → sells only + Discord alert |

## Backtest of the final design (net of 0.10%/side costs)

| | 2007–2026 | 1997–2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Annual return | 4.9% (SPY 11.2%, 60/40 8.2%) | 6.4% (SPY 9.7%, 60/40 7.9%) |
| Sharpe | 0.55 (SPY 0.56, 60/40 0.64) | 0.69 (SPY 0.46, 60/40 0.55) |
| Worst drawdown | −12.8% (SPY −55%, 60/40 −33%) | −12.6% (SPY −55%, 60/40 −32%) |
| Chance its Sharpe beats SPY's (bootstrap) | 44% | 90% |

Replay of the live code over 2010–2026 against a simulated broker tracks the model within 0.15% a year (4.07% vs 4.10% a year).

**Expect it to:**

- trail SPY in most years (it beat SPY in 21–31% of calendar years)
- earn about half of SPY's long-run return
- avoid the deep crashes
