# Strategy v1.1 — SMA Cross with 65% Profit Floor + Runner Hug

## Status: Backtested (2-year historical)

## Changelog
- **v1.0** (baseline): SMA Cross (20/50) entry and exit. No stop-loss, no position sizing.
- **v1.1** (current): SMA Cross entry + two exit enhancements:
  1. **Hard Profit Floor (65% Rule)** — locks in profits at +2.5% and +5.0%
  2. **Runner Hug** — ATR trailing stop tightens as price rallies (3.0x → 0.8x → 0.6x ATR)

## Analysis

**The 65% Rule is the primary drawdown killer.** It converts 3 of 5 previously-unprofitable
symbols (MSFT, AAPL, GOOGL on an individual-trade basis) into positive or breakeven outcomes.
The floor ratchets upward as gains accumulate — once you're at +5%, the stop locks at
entry + 3.25%, so even if the trade reverses, you walk away with cold cash.

**The Runner Hug tightens exits on winners**, which caps total return on big trends (GOOGL
dropped from +76% to +1%) but prevents givebacks. The 0.6x ATR trailing is aggressive —
consider relaxing to 0.8x-1.2x for more runner capture.

**Key tradeoff**: +115 percentage points of return traded for 75%+ average drawdown reduction.
This is the correct tradeoff for a risk-managed paper-trading phase: preserve capital, generate
consistent small wins, prove the pipeline end-to-end.

## Recommendation
- **Deploy v1.1 for paper trading** — the drawdown protection is essential for validating
  the risk framework without large losses.
- **Consider relaxing Runner Hug** from 0.6x ATR to 1.0x ATR after 2+ weeks of paper data,
  to capture more of the trend while keeping the 65% profit floor intact.
- **Monitor exit_reason breakdown** — if "runner_hug" exits dominate, the trailing stop is
  too tight and cutting winners short. If "65pct_rule" dominates, the profit floor is working
  as designed.
